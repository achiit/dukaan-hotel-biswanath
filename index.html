A roadside kitchen you can trust

Hotel Biswanath started on this stretch near Dhaipur, with my father cooking and me carrying plates. The red front and the small Shiva shrine at the door are the first things people remember about us.

We cook to order, so a plate takes 15 to 20 minutes. Our staff wear red and know most regular faces by name. If you drive this road often, we hope you stop twice.

Call before you come if you are a big group. We will keep a table ready.

  • Veg and non-veg under one roof, from ₹30 to ₹400
  • Half and full plates, so one person or a family can order
  • Cooked fresh, 15–20 minutes, printed on the menu
  • Open late, kitchen closes at 11:30 pm
  • Three phone numbers, someone always picks up
